Sweats for Android

Beta on Google Play · Android 8.0 or newer

Sweats for Android is in beta on Google Play. Joining takes two minutes. You get the app from the Play Store and it updates by itself.

How to join

  1. Join the Sweats group. Open the group and tap Join group. Use the Google account that's signed in to the Play Store on your phone.
  2. Become a tester. Open the tester page with that same account and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install. On the tester page, tap Download it on Google Play, then Install.
  4. Open Sweats and sign in. Your account works the same as on iPhone.

Start: join the Sweats group

Have the Sweats APK from this page? Uninstall it first, then install from Google Play. The APK can't update to the Play version. Your workouts and posts are saved to your account, so nothing is lost.

Help us launch

Google opens Sweats to everyone on the Play Store once enough testers have stayed in the beta for two weeks. Please keep the app installed and stay opted in, and train with it if you can. Every workout helps.

Something not working?

  • "App not available" on the tester page: you haven't joined the group yet, or you joined with a different Google account. Join with the account on your phone, wait a minute, then open the tester page again.
  • Tester page shows a different account: tap your profile picture at the top right and switch to the account you used for the group.
  • "App not installed": an older Sweats APK is still on the phone. Uninstall it, then install from Play.

Still stuck? Email support@sweats.in with your phone model and a screenshot.
