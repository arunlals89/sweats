Sweats for Android

On Google Play · Android 8.0 or newer

Sweats is free on Google Play. Install it, sign in, and your account works the same as on iPhone. It reads steps and workouts from Health Connect, and updates itself from the Play Store.

Get it on Google Play

Installed the Sweats APK from this site earlier? Uninstall it first, then install from Google Play. The APK can't update to the Play version. Your workouts and posts are saved to your account, so nothing is lost.

Something not working?

  • "App not installed": an older Sweats APK is still on the phone. Uninstall it, then install from Play.
  • Can't find Sweats in the Play Store: use the button above, or search for "Sweats Social Fitness".

Still stuck? Email support@sweats.in with your phone model and a screenshot.
