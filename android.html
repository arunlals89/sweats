Sweats for Android

Version 1.0.6 · 175 MB · Android 8.0 or newer

Download Sweats APK

Open this page on your Android phone and tap the button. Then follow the steps below. It takes about two minutes.

Why an APK?

Sweats isn't public on the Google Play Store yet. Right now it's in Google Play testing, which means joining a group, signing up as a tester and waiting on Google's review before the app shows up. The APK skips all of that so you can start training today.

It's the real thing. This is the exact build Google Play delivers to our testers, signed by Google Play. Nothing is added or changed.

How to install

  1. Download. Tap Download Sweats APK above. If Chrome warns that the file might be harmful, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open it. When the download finishes, tap Open. You can also find sweats-android.apk in your Files app under Downloads.
  3. Allow the install. Android asks if Chrome (or Files) may install apps. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Install. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect pops up, tap More details → Install anyway. It shows this for any app that didn't come from the Play Store.
  5. Open Sweats and sign in. Your account works the same as on iPhone or the Play version.

Getting updates

APK installs don't update on their own. When a new version is out, come back to this page and install the new APK over the old one. Your account and data stay put. Once Sweats is live on the Play Store, installing it from there updates this same app.

Want automatic updates now? Join the Sweats group with the Google account on your phone, then open the tester page and tap Become a tester.

Something not working?

  • "App not installed": you probably have an older Sweats beta that was installed from a different file. Uninstall it, then install this one. Your workouts and posts are saved to your account, so nothing is lost.
  • Nothing happens when you tap the file: on Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phones, open the APK from the File Manager app instead of the browser.
  • "There was a problem parsing the package": your phone needs Android 8.0 or newer, or the download didn't finish. Delete the file and download it again on Wi-Fi.

Still stuck? Email support@sweats.in with your phone model and a screenshot.
