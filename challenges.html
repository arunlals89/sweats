Group Fitness Challenges That Track Themselves

Most group challenges die in the group chat. Someone has to keep the spreadsheet, people forget to post their numbers, and by week two nobody is checking. In Sweats you set the goal once, invite your people, and then just train: every workout you record counts itself, and the leaderboard updates as people move.

How a Sweats challenge works

  1. Pick the game. Steps, distance, workout minutes or calories.
  2. Set a target. A daily number or a total to hit before the challenge ends.
  3. Invite your people. A handful of friends, your office floor, or everyone in your tribe.
  4. Train as usual. Workouts you record in Sweats, plus steps and workouts from Apple Health on iPhone, count on their own. There are no check-ins and no honour system.
  5. Watch the standings move. Everyone in the challenge sees the same live leaderboard, updating as people train.

Why a leaderboard that fills itself works

Group challenges usually fail for boring reasons: logging is a chore, goals are out of reach for half the group, and nobody can see where they stand. We wrote about that pattern in why most group fitness challenges fail by day 3.

  • No manual logging. The number comes from tracking, so nobody debates whose tracker is lying.
  • Visible standings. You always know who is one walk ahead of you.
  • Shared activity. One workout counts toward every challenge you’re in, your duels and this week’s league table at the same time.

Let the AI build it

Pick a type and a few basics, and the AI Challenge Builder writes the title, the goal, day-by-day milestones, tips and hashtags, tuned to the fitness level you choose. You review it before anything is created. It’s free to try every day and unlimited on Sweats Pro. More about the AI coach.

Weekly leagues: the challenge that never ends

Alongside the challenges you create, every activity earns Health Points that drop you into a live weekly league against people training at your level. Finish in the top 20% and you promote, from Bronze through Silver, Gold and Platinum to Diamond. You can see the promotion and relegation lines all week, and it resets every Monday. Duels for when it’s just the two of you

Want something faster than a week-long challenge? Tap Beat this on a friend’s workout and it becomes a private 1-on-1 race to their number. Duels are free, unlimited and don’t count against your challenge limit. How fitness duels work.

Fitness challenge FAQ

Are challenges free?

Yes. Joining challenges is free and unlimited. On the free plan you can run one active challenge of your own at a time; Sweats Pro removes that cap. Duels don’t count toward it.

Do I have to log my workouts by hand?

No. Workouts you record in Sweats count automatically, and on iPhone Sweats also reads steps and workouts from Apple Health. The leaderboard fills itself.

How do leagues and Health Points work?

Every activity you record earns Health Points. Those points place you in a weekly league cohort — Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum or Diamond — alongside people at a similar level. Every Monday the top 20% of each cohort promote and the bottom 20% relegate, and you can see exactly which side of the line you’re on all week. Sweats Pro earns points at 2×.

Can I run a challenge for my whole tribe?

Yes. A challenge can be just you and a few friends, or the whole tribe’s week, with its own leaderboard everyone in it can see.

Which phones does Sweats work on?

Sweats is on the App Store for iPhone now and reads activity from Apple Health. On Android? Be one of our beta testers: grab the APK and help shape Sweats before it hits Google Play.
