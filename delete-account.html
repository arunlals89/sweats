Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your Sweats account and what happens to your data when you do — whether you still have the app installed or not. If you only want to remove specific data and keep your account, see Request Data Deletion instead.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

This is the fastest way and takes effect immediately.

  1. Open the Sweats app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account and tap it.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account is deactivated immediately and permanently deleted from our systems.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, can't sign in, or lost access to your phone number, email privacy@sweats.in from any email address with the subject line "Delete my account", and include:

  • The phone number you used to sign up for Sweats
  • Your Sweats username, if you remember it

We'll verify the request and delete your account within 7 business days, and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — name, username, bio, avatar, phone number
  • Your activity and workout history, including recorded routes
  • Your posts, comments, and reactions
  • Your messages (your side of every conversation)
  • Your health/fitness data synced from Apple Health or Health Connect
  • Your location data (live-activity history, nearby-alert settings)
  • Your subscription and purchase records held by us (billing history remains with Apple/Google as the store of record)

What may be kept

Some data can't be deleted outright because it's shared with other people who are still using the app:

  • Communities, challenges, or events you created stay active for their other members, with your name replaced by a generic label (e.g. "Deleted user")
  • Comments or messages that are part of a shared thread with another user remain visible to that user, with your identity removed
  • Reports you filed or were named in are kept for moderation and safety records, with your identity removed where you were the reporter

We may also retain limited records where required for legal, security, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes, for as long as applicable law requires. Deletion may take up to 30 days to fully propagate across backups.
