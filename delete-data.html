Request Data Deletion

Last updated: September 22, 2026

You don't have to delete your entire Sweats account to remove specific data. This page explains how to request deletion of individual data types while keeping your account and the rest of your activity intact. If you'd rather delete everything, see Delete Your Account instead.

What you can request deletion of

  • Your GPS route and location history from recorded activities
  • Health and fitness data synced from Apple Health or Health Connect
  • Uploaded contacts used for friend suggestions
  • Specific photos or videos you've posted
  • Specific posts, comments, or messages
  • Your saved location used for nearby-user alerts

How to request it

  1. Email privacy@sweats.in with the subject line "Delete my data".
  2. Tell us your Sweats username or the phone number on your account, so we can verify it's you.
  3. Tell us exactly which data type(s) from the list above you want deleted.

We'll verify the request and complete it within 7 business days, and confirm by email once it's done. Your account stays active and everything else is unaffected.

What may be kept

If the data you're asking to delete is part of a shared thread with another user — for example a message in a conversation, or a comment on someone else's post — we can remove it from your side and strip your identifying information, but we can't reach into the other participant's copy of that conversation. We may also retain limited records where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, for as long as applicable law requires. Deletion may take up to 30 days to fully propagate across backups.

Questions

Contact us at privacy@sweats.in or +91 96338 66066. See our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle your data.
