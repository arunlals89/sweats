Everything you need

All of your training.
Finally in one place.

Right now your workouts live in a tracker, your crew lives in a group chat, and the leaderboard lives in someone’s head. Sweats puts all three on the same screen.

Routes you can actually see

Every GPS workout posts with a real map of where you went — not a row of numbers. Distance, steps, pace and calories ride along with it.

Go live mid-workout

Friends watch your route and stats move in real time, send cheers that float up your screen, and get the milestone the second you hit it.

Duels — “Beat this”

See a friend’s run you fancy your chances against? Tap Beat this. It becomes a private 1-on-1 race to the number, on your clock. Winner gets bragging rights and a share card.

Weekly leagues

Every workout earns Health Points that drop you into a live league. Finish in the top 20% and you promote — Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond. Resets every Monday.

Challenges that count themselves

Steps, distance, minutes, calories — pick a goal, invite your people, and the leaderboard fills from your recorded activity. No check-in spreadsheets, no honour system.

Tribes for your crew

Build a group around your sport, your city or your gym — with its own feed, events, challenges and members. Up to 1,000 of them on Pro.

Events & meetups

Post a Saturday trail run or a tribe gym session, see who’s in, and find what’s happening near you in Discover. First one you join unlocks a badge.

An AI coach that texts first

It reads your real training history, then messages you when your streak is about to break, when you’ve had a strong week, or when you’re losing a duel with hours left.

Train with partners

Add up to five people before you start. They get an invite, their live status sits next to yours mid-session, and everyone gets tagged on the post automatically.

Streaks, badges & freezes

A streak that holds you honest, badges worth unlocking, a weekly recap of what you actually did — and a freeze for the day life gets in the way.

Find people nearby

Get a heads-up when someone else who trains is within about a kilometre of you. Foreground only, off with one tap, and never shown as a live map of you.

Messages & QR connect

Plan the session, talk trash, share the win. Request-gated DMs so strangers can’t pile in — or just scan someone’s QR code and you’re connected on the spot.

Sweat Gems

Your people

Tribes

A group chat that keeps score

Start a tribe for your run club, your gym, your office floor. It gets its own feed, its own events, its own challenges and its own leaderboard — up to 1,000 members on Pro. Public ones show up in Discover so people can find you.

Events

Sunday, 6am, the usual spot

Create a session with a time, a place and a cover photo, and watch the RSVPs come in. Browse what’s happening near you in Discover, and your profile keeps a rail of everything you’re hosting and everything you’re going to.

Finding each other

Three ways to add someone

Scan their QR code in person. Sync your contacts to see who’s already here. Or leave Nearby Alerts on and get a nudge when another member is training within a kilometre of you — foreground only, off in one tap.
