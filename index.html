Compete

The gentlest pressure there is: someone you know, one number ahead.

Motivation is unreliable. A friend who just posted 8.4km and a league table that resets on Monday are not.

Duels

Two taps to turn their workout into your problem.

Anyone’s activity post in your feed carries a Beat this button. Tap it, pick how long you’ve got, and it becomes a private head-to-head — just the two of you, first to the number wins.

  • ✓ Sweats picks the fair metric automatically — distance, steps, minutes or calories
  • ✓ You get told the moment the lead changes, and when time’s nearly up
  • ✓ Win and you get a ready-made share card. Losing quietly is not an option.
  • ✓ Free and unlimited — duel as much as you like

Leagues

Five tiers. One week. No hiding at the bottom.

Every activity earns Health Points, and those points drop you into a live weekly table against people training at your level. Finish in the top fifth and you climb. Coast, and you don’t.

  • ✓ Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond, each with its own banner
  • ✓ You can see the promotion and relegation lines all week — no surprises on Monday
  • ✓ Global leaderboards and per-tribe rankings alongside it
  • ✓ Same Health Points for everyone — Pro can’t buy a faster climb
