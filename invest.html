The opportunity

Tracking is solved. Showing up isn't.

Every fitness app can log a run. Almost none keep people coming back. Retention is the whole game, and retention is social. That's the wedge.

A real behaviour loop

Duels, leagues and live cheers give people a reason to open the app when they aren't working out — then a reason to go train. Competition is the retention engine tracking apps lack.

Built-in virality

You can't duel alone. Every challenge, tribe and league invites a friend, so each active user pulls in the next — community is the product, not a bolt-on.

AI as a moat, not a feature

Our coach reads actual training data to message users first, draft challenges and keep them accountable — the kind of 1:1 attention that used to need a human trainer.
