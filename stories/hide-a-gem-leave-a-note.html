Hide a Gem, Leave a Note: The Tiny Surprise Waiting on Someone’s Walk

October 24, 2026 · 4 min read

Somewhere on a park loop, a gem is sitting on the path. Nobody can see who left it. Then a runner comes round the bend, their phone buzzes, and a Pulse Ruby lands in their Vault, along with a line from a total stranger:

“Halfway up the hill. Don’t you dare walk now.”

That’s the whole idea. With Sweats you can now hide one of your Sweat Gems on the route and leave a note with it. Whoever walks past next finds both.

Who finds it? That’s the fun part.

It could be a stranger out on their first 5K, who needed exactly your three words at exactly that corner. Or you could drop it on the loop your friend runs every single morning and let them stumble on it tomorrow at 6 am. They won’t see it coming, and they’ll know it was you.

A few notes people will leave:

  • “Best chai is 200 m to your left. You’ve earned it.”
  • “If you found this before 7 am, you’re a legend.”
  • “Happy birthday, Meera. Yes, I planned this.”
  • “Last km. Sprint it.”

Somebody’s next walk could use a surprise.

How to hide a gem

  1. Head out on a walk, run or hike in Sweats, the same as always.
  2. Open your Gem Vault from your profile and tap the gem you want to give away.
  3. Write a note (optional, up to 140 characters) and tap Leave it here. The gem is placed exactly where you’re standing.

It stays on the map until someone walks past it. No timer, no expiry. The first person to come by collects it, sees who left it and reads your note.

A real gift, not a free one

A gem you hide really leaves your Vault, along with the Sweat Points it was worth. A Sweat Drop costs you 1, a Burn Stone costs you 20. That’s what makes it mean something: you’re giving away a gem you walked for, not one the app minted for free.

Built so your home stays private

  • Never near your start. You can’t hide a gem within 200 m of where your walk began, so nobody marks their front door on the map by accident.
  • Only on the move. Gems can only be hidden during a walk, run or hike, never from your sofa.
  • You can’t find your own. A gem you hid never shows on your own map. It’s for someone else.
  • Blocked means blocked. Gems from people you’ve blocked (or who blocked you) never show up.

Quick answers

Can I hide a gem for one specific friend?

Not directly. Whoever passes first gets it. The trick is to hide it somewhere only they walk.

Does the finder know it was me?

Yes. They see your username along with your note. Leave something nice.

How many can I hide?

Up to five a day.

Do I need Sweats Pro?

No. Hiding and finding gems is free for everyone. More on how Sweat Gems work.
