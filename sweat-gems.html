Sweat Gems: Treasure on the Routes You Already Walk

Most walks end the same way: you get home, you save it, done. Sweat Gems give the route itself something to find. Gems are hidden along real paths near you. Start a walk, run or hike, pass close to one, and it’s yours, right there on the spot, with a buzz in your pocket.

It’s a small thing that changes the walk. You take the left turn because there’s a Stamina Spire down that road. You add the extra loop because a Burn Stone is sitting at the far end of the park. The kilometres go up and it doesn’t feel like effort.

How collecting works

  1. Start a walk, run or hike. Pick it in Sweats and press start, as you normally would.
  2. Look at the map. Gems near you show up as small sparkling markers on your live route map, so you can see where they are before you set off.
  3. Walk past one. Get within about 25 metres and the gem is collected instantly. Your phone buzzes, and a counter on the map ticks up as the gem flies into it.
  4. Save as usual. You don’t have to save the walk to keep a gem, it’s already yours. When you do save, your post shows a small 💎 count that shines once in your friends’ feeds.

Three gems, three rarities

Every gem adds to your balance. The rarer the gem, the more it’s worth:

  • Sweat Drop (common), worth 1. The one you’ll see most often.
  • Stamina Spire (rare), worth 5. Fewer of these around, worth going out of your way for.
  • Burn Stone (epic), worth 20. The big find. Worth a detour.

Each gem can be collected once per person, so a gem you’ve found disappears from your map, while your friends can still go and find it themselves.

Where gems come from

We place gems along routes the Sweats community actually uses: parks, promenades and popular loops, plus special drops for events. New gems appear over time, so a route you know well can surprise you again. And some gems were hidden by other walkers, each with a note waiting inside.

Hide a gem, leave a note

Here’s the part we love. On your walk, open your Vault, tap a gem and leave it right where you’re standing, with a note for whoever finds it. Then walk away and let the route do the rest.

Maybe a stranger on their first 5K picks it up at exactly the corner where they needed it. Maybe it’s the friend who runs that loop every morning at 6, and gets a surprise from you before sunrise. You won’t know who. They’ll know it was you.

Spend them: the Gem Vault

Your collection lives in the Gem Vault, one tap from the top of your profile. It shows your balance, how many of each gem you’ve found and your recent finds, each linked to the walk it came from.

With Sweats Pro, you can spend 15 gems on a streak freeze, which keeps your streak alive on a rest day. Unlike the freezes you get each month, bought freezes don’t expire at the end of the month, and you can hold up to three. Collecting gems is free for everyone; spending them is a Pro perk. New to freezes? Read why missing a day is OK.

Fair play and privacy

  • On foot only. Gems count on walks, runs and hikes at a real on-foot pace. Cycling past or driving through doesn’t collect anything.
  • Your post shows a count, never a place. Friends see that you found 3 gems, not where you found them.
  • Nobody sees who collected a gem. A gem on the map never shows who has or hasn’t found it.

Sweat Gems FAQ

Are Sweat Gems free?

Collecting is. Every Sweats user sees gems on the map, collects them and shows them off on their posts. Spending gems on streak freezes is a Sweats Pro perk.

Do I have to save my walk to keep a gem?

No. A gem is collected the moment you pass it. Saving the walk just adds the 💎 count to your post.

Can I collect gems while cycling or driving?

No. Gems only count on walks, runs and hikes, at an on-foot pace.

Can I find the same gem twice?

No. Each gem can be collected once per person. Once you have it, it disappears from your map.

What can I spend gems on?

Streak freezes, at 15 gems each, with up to three held at a time. Spending gems needs Sweats Pro. More ways to use them are on the way.

Can I hide a gem for someone else?

Yes. During a walk, tap a gem in your Vault, add a note and leave it where you are. Whoever walks past next collects it and reads your note. To surprise a friend, hide it on a route they walk.
